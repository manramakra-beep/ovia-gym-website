ABOUT US

Built by people
who lift.

OVIA GYM started in Pollachi with one idea: training should be honest. No overcrowded floors, no equipment queues, no trend-chasing classes that disappear in a month. Just proper coaching, real equipment, and a space that takes your training as seriously as you do.

Every coach on our floor is certified and trains alongside our members — not behind a desk. Whether you're here for your first deadlift or your hundredth, the standard doesn't change.
